[h1]奶龙替换以撒[/h1]
A fan-made yellow NaiLong / Milk Frog-inspired skin for normal Isaac in The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+.

[h2]Appearance[/h2]
[list]
[*]A small smile at rest, a wide laugh while shooting, and four-direction walking with visible footsteps.
[*]Optional movement-facing lets the head follow movement when you are not shooting. Breathing and blinking idle animations settle after you stop.
[*]Technology eyes, Brimstone horns and homing heart eyes can combine. Brimstone, Tech X and Chocolate Milk have charging expressions.
[*]Decorative wings follow native flight. The body uses a separate flying costume even when decorative wings are disabled.
[*]After completing a Boss fight, NaiLong holds his belly and laughs with audio. Multi-boss fights wait for the room to finish; Boss Rush celebrates after the final wave.
[*]The celebration lasts up to five seconds and yields to damage, item holding, room changes and special actions. Returning to a completed Boss room does not repeat it.
[/list]

[h2]Changes in 1.4.5[/h2]
[list]
[*]Use the native Sprite pointer hash instead of comparing Lua wrapper objects. Stable walking and shooting no longer repeatedly remove/re-add the head and body costumes, which resets their independent animation clocks. Actual native sprite replacement still triggers recovery.
[*]Track the weapon appearances hidden by this skin and restore still-owned item/effect costumes when Isaac changes to another character or a co-op ghost. Removed items are not restored.
[*]Queue Boss celebration while controls are locked or cooling down. Start after control returns; interrupt an active laugh if controls are locked again.
[*]Added an independent movement-facing switch, enabled by default. Disabling it preserves native aiming while idle breathing remains separately configurable.
[*]Added Reset preferences in Mod Config Menu. It restores this skin's settings and saves them without changing items or character stats.
[*]Preserved all 1.4.3 sprite, animation and audio assets unchanged.
[/list]

[h2]Changes in 1.4.3[/h2]
[list]
[*]Added dedicated ground/flying body selection to address missing bodies after gaining flight. Temporary flight ending restores the ground costume.
[*]Repacked both belly-laugh phases in all four directions and paired the base celebration head with its body phase.
[*]Corrected mismatched left/right shooting profiles and kept the back-facing head consistent between firing frames.
[*]Rebuilt the walking cycle with a stable torso and three leg poses. Unchanged walking or shooting no longer reloads textures every frame.
[*]Preserved native pose, scale and playback during appearance refreshes; retained item expressions, idle animation and laugh preferences.
[/list]

[h2]Use and settings[/h2]
Enable one copy of this mod, fully restart the game after updating, and select normal Isaac.
Optional Mod Config Menu: category [b]NaiLong[/b]. Toggle movement-facing, weapon faces, homing eyes, wings, idle animation, charging faces, Boss laughter and laugh audio. Reset preferences is under Settings. Adjust laugh volume and duration (0.5-5 seconds).

[h2]Scope[/h2]
This is a cosmetic skin. It does not change health, stats, item abilities, inventory, attacks, controls, hitboxes or the HUD. Other characters keep their native appearance. Mega Mush and incompatible full-body sprite layouts temporarily use their native visuals. Other mods replacing the same player head/body may conflict.
Visual and Lua regression checks for this update were performed offline; native in-game flying and celebration rendering has not yet been confirmed.
